Executive Summary:
This comprehensive inspection report details the condition of the property at 123 Main Street. Overall, the property is in good condition with these exceptions.

Inspection Details:

1. Exterior

   - Driveway: Asphalt driveway with some cracking, needs sealing
   - Landscaping: Well-maintained, proper drainage away from house

2. Interior
   - Living Room: Good condition, no issues noted

   - Bathrooms (2): Good condition, no leaks detected
   - Basement: Minor water damage in northeast corner, recommend waterproofing

3. Systems

Recommendations:
1. Replace roof within next 3-5 years
2. Waterproof basement in northeast corner
3. Upgrade electrical panel
4. Seal driveway cracks

Estimated Costs:

- Basement waterproofing: $2,500-$4,000
- Electrical panel upgrade: $1,500-$2,500
